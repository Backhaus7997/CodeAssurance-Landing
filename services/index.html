Services

QA services

Code Assurance provides software QA services for product teams: test automation, manual testing, dedicated QA engineers who join your team, an AI QA agent that works on your codebase, and mobile, API, performance and security testing. Every engagement starts with a short call and an assessment, and is shaped around your product, not around a template.

  • Test automation services →

    Test automation services in your framework: we build a suite from zero, extend the one you have or keep it maintained, with an AI QA agent as an add-on.

  • Performance testing and audit services →

    Performance testing and audits for web apps and APIs: baselines for your critical user journeys, the bottlenecks behind them and a plan backed by measured runs.

  • Security testing services →

    Manual security testing focused on what your next release puts at risk: attack surface mapping, validated findings with evidence, fix guidance and retests.

  • Code quality review services →

    Code quality reviews that set quality gates matching real risk, find the hotspots that cause regressions and order your technical debt by business impact.

  • Manual QA testing services →

    Manual QA testing by senior engineers: functional, regression and exploratory testing of your web and mobile apps, with bugs reported in your own tracker.

  • Dedicated QA engineers for your team →

    Dedicated QA engineers who join your team: manual or automation testers in your ceremonies and tools, with US time overlap, month-to-month and no long contract.

  • AI QA agent for your test suite →

    Our AI QA agent maps your codebase, writes tests in your framework and repairs broken locators, with every change reviewed by a senior QA engineer first.

  • Mobile app testing services for Android and iOS →

    Mobile app testing for Android and iOS on real devices and emulators: functional, regression and compatibility testing across OS versions, plus Appium.

  • API testing services →

    API testing services: functional and contract tests for your endpoints and integrations, data checks in SQL, and automated runs in your CI pipeline.
