05 / FAQs

FAQs

Is manual testing still worth it if we have automated tests?+

Yes. Automation is best at repeating known checks; people are better at new features, usability problems and the unexpected. Most products need both, and we help decide what stays manual and what gets automated.

What do you need from us to start?+

Access to a test environment, your tracker and whatever describes the product: stories, acceptance criteria or a walkthrough with someone who knows it. In the first week we map the critical flows and agree on how bugs are reported.

Do you test mobile apps too?+

Yes, on Android and iOS, on real devices and emulators. Our mobile app testing page explains how we choose devices and OS versions.

Can manual test cases be automated later?+

Yes. Stable, repetitive checks are the best candidates. We flag them as we go, and our automation engineers or our AI QA agent can turn them into tests in your framework.

How do you report bugs?+

In your tracker, with steps to reproduce, expected and actual results, screenshots or video, logs when they help, and a severity agreed with your team.
