Privacy policy

What this covers

Code Assurance provides software quality assurance services and is operated by Backhaustin S.A.S. (CUIT 30-71929587-4), a company registered in Argentina, which is responsible for the information described here.

This page explains what happens with the information you send us through this website.

What we collect

When you use the contact form we receive your name, work email, company, phone number if you add it, and your message. We don't ask for anything else.

This site does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security.

Why we use it

Only to answer you and, if you want, to talk about working together. We don't sell your information or share it for marketing.

Who processes it

The contact form is delivered by Formspree (formspree.io), which stores submissions on our behalf. If you book a call, the booking page is run by the scheduling provider and its own privacy policy applies.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it to follow up with you. We delete it when you ask.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent us, write to martin.backhaus@code-assurance.com.
