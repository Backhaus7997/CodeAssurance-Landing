02 / Process

Assess, plan, deliver, stabilize

The same four steps on every engagement, sized to your product and your release rhythm.

01

Assess

Risk assessment and a reality check of your product, your CI and where things fail.

You get

  • Risk map by module
  • Top issues and quick wins
  • Prioritized test backlog by level: smoke, regression, E2E, API
02

Plan

Test strategy, coverage plan and quality gates you can measure.

You get

  • A 1–2 page test strategy, ready to execute
  • Risk-to-coverage matrix
  • CI/CD gates: what blocks a merge or a release, and who owns it
03

Deliver

Testing scoped to each change, and automation where it pays off.

You get

  • Release and PR test reports with evidence
  • An automation increment with maintenance rules
  • A pre-deploy exit checklist
04

Stabilize

Pipeline visibility, flaky-test triage and CI hardening.

You get

  • Stability dashboard: flakes, failures, runtime
  • A decision, root cause and owner for each flaky test
  • CI hardening summary, before and after
