QA guides

Practical guides for engineering and product leaders who want fewer bugs in production and calmer releases. They cover how to hire a QA company, when to outsource QA or build a team, how to improve QA in your company and what drives the cost of outsourcing. Each one is useful whether or not you work with us.

  • 01

    How to hire a QA company: a buyer's checklist →

    A practical checklist for hiring a QA company: when it makes sense, the three engagement models, questions to ask, red flags and what the first month shows.

  • 02

    QA outsourcing vs. an in-house QA team →

    QA outsourcing or an in-house QA team? Compare time to start, product knowledge, control, flexibility and what drives cost, then use a checklist to decide.

  • 03

    How to improve QA in your company →

    A practical plan to improve QA in your company: find where bugs escape, map risk, test at the right level, automate critical flows and fix flaky tests.

  • 04

    What QA outsourcing costs depend on →

    What drives the cost of QA outsourcing: engagement model, scope, platforms, the state of your test suite, team dedication and tools, and how to get a quote.
